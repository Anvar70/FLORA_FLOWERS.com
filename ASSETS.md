# Flower assets
All website images and flags are stored locally; browsing the site makes no third-party image/font requests.

- static/images/hero.jpg and hero.png: generated using the built-in image generation tool for this project. Prompt: “A luxurious natural loosely arranged bouquet of blush pink garden roses, cream ranunculus, delicate white flowers and fresh eucalyptus in a matte ivory ceramic vase on a warm limestone table. Warm pale sage studio background, soft morning side light, refined editorial botanical photography, realistic petals, entire bouquet in frame. No text, logos, people or watermark.” Use: local demonstration hero/product image.
